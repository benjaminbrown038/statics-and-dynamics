# Further study

The runnable examples and lesson explanations are original teaching material.
These primary educational sources provide fuller treatments:

- [MIT OpenCourseWare — Engineering Dynamics](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/2-003sc-engineering-dynamics-fall-2011/): particle and rigid-body motion, angular momentum, work-energy, and vibration.
- [MIT OpenCourseWare — Mechanics & Materials I](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/2-001-mechanics-materials-i-fall-2006/): forces in structures, geometry, and the relation between loads and deformation.

Links were checked during preparation. Numeric baseline cases are illustrative;
they are not material allowables, measured mechanism performance, or aircraft
qualification data.
